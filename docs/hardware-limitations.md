# J614s hardware limitations

These notes apply to the combined forked stack described in [status](status.md). Tests on this Mac do not qualify an unmodified upstream installation or other hardware. The [upstream work index](../experiments/j614s-upstream/README.md) links the changes under review.

- **GPU:** The desktop and short rendering and compute workloads work. The proposed 16-core firmware buffer layout and preemption remain unverified; queue overlap or changed buffers alone do not establish a context switch. The [context observation experiment](../experiments/j614s-gpu-context/README.md) records the useful follow-up and its limits.
- **Speakers:** Keep the speaker card and amplifiers disabled. J614s-specific protection parameters, the playback and voltage/current sense mapping, and fault handling are not qualified. Similarity to J616s or J314 wiring and profiles is not sufficient to establish a safe profile. See [Aurora Linux #406](https://github.com/aurora-silicon/linux/pull/406) and [#419](https://github.com/aurora-silicon/linux/pull/419) for related protection work.
- **Thermal:** The GPU `power_allocator` can limit CPU power well below the GPU target with the inherited single passive trip. [Linux #852](https://github.com/aurora-silicon/linux/pull/852) proposes an experimental two-trip policy; its 100 °C switch-on, hot-load response, sustainable power and J616s behavior are not qualified. On this J614s the `TVMR` and `TVMr` readings rose above 100 °C under some workloads; their physical meaning, calibration and safe limits remain unknown. Do not treat previous experimental guard thresholds as hardware ratings.
- **Storage handoff:** The source review identified possible NVMe DMA buffer lifetime concerns after timeouts, including failed controller reset. These are not reproduced failures and a successful boot does not prove all failure paths safe. Review the pinned U-Boot and EFI handoff before changing reservations or boot components; see [U-Boot #8](https://github.com/aurora-silicon/u-boot/pull/8) and [#16](https://github.com/aurora-silicon/u-boot/pull/16).
- **USB and displays:** USB 3 reads passed on all three ports in both orientations with one drive. HDMI worked with an older loader, but HDMI with the later handoff and USB-C display output are untested. Write integrity, hubs, 10 Gb/s and suspend/resume remain unqualified.
- **Touch ID and input:** Touch ID worked on the combined stack, including after a reboot; it was not independently tested in the default-only trial. The J614s MTP firmware file named by the device tree is not installed. Keyboard and trackpad worked through the retained session, but recovery from a failed handoff is unqualified; keep an external mouse available.
- **Camera, microphone and SD:** A live camera image, clear microphone recording, and SD card mounting and editing were confirmed on combined trials. Camera tuning, microphone channel characterization, SD sustained I/O and suspend/resume remain unqualified. The replacement PR-stack kernel has not been tested with wired headphones.

Historical reports and their trial details are available in the [repository history at `381d735`](https://github.com/mchalunderscore/M4RCH/tree/381d735). Machine-local firmware, test evidence and recovery archives are not contained in Git.
