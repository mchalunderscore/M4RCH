# Independent review of the retained GFX power-off candidate

2026-10-09. Source, build identity and offline replay review only. No module load, runtime-PM operation, MMIO, device-tree change, reboot, backing release or hardware trial was performed by this reviewer.

**Result: no unresolved implementation findings in the frozen changed code.** The distinct candidate is qualified for its narrow retained, no-work power-off observation. This does not establish physical OFF, drain, safe teardown, restart or rendering. It grants no action on the currently sealed native-reset owner.

The concrete candidate is `out/gfx-poweroff-scope/candidate`. Its owner module SHA-256 is `4b4914b677c15b55718e6e96f4d6683a0dd295f4337659aff709a8ffef931be0`; its `asc-root-envelope/build-receipt.json` SHA-256 is `8644ae827a06da50d9793199a7c780305cff85abb9c9e826cde41b967971f471`. The review binds the source qualification `2a59e00ca72325d5e426859d83f00ea5e2bce80149b4f7829419fb1513d8c726` and wrapper qualification `e47b4d1bd05dcaca77b8b45323605a9780ffdb53a91c8d15794b7106d5633e48` to that build. All 59 recorded input hashes were checked. The machine-readable companion records them and the 26 binding checks.

The candidate retains all 174 owned pages, its installed graph and device context, ASC/mailbox mappings and reservations, private platform consumer, and module pin. Its retained predicate deliberately does not require a live PM reference after OFF. Normal unload remains prevented by the existing module pin. Cleanup remains compiled for startup failures and the allocation-only shadow; the terminal command does not call it. The ordinary cold startup admits only gate 1 without a resident session or gate 2 with one, and rejects shutdown, warm restart and backing release. The prepared graph and backing were not changed.

Under the shared graph mutex, packet-free `stop` seals host service and attempts START clear at most once, followed by fresh START-clear and native idle observations. OFF admission additionally requires the held, enabled and active private PM consumer with usage count exactly 1 and no runtime error; no work submitted; all 36 work cursor words empty; no active record among 256 TA records; phase 0 or 2; and equal control cursors within 32 through 128. The initial driver-owned PMGR observation must be valid, report the domain ON, and satisfy `(word & 0x80001cff) == 0xff`.

Every explicit `gfx-poweroff` request consumes the permanent gate, including an admission refusal and a nonresident request. On admitted execution, ownership of the sole PM reference is cleared before exactly one `pm_runtime_put_sync_suspend` call. No compensating get, second put, reset pulse, restart or release occurs. The receive worker shares the mutex, observes the permanent failure seal and cannot drain messages or reschedule after the operation. Cancellation is intentionally nonsynchronous while holding that mutex.

After gate consumption, diagnostic routes use only the existing PMGR snapshot API, software PM accounting and retained DRAM. They do not dereference ASC, SGX or mailbox mappings. The kernel's guarded `stop` route also remains free of MMIO after the gate; the wrapper permits only `sample` and `gfx-snapshot` after any attempted or uncertain OFF. The sysfs RAM snapshots and receive journal copy retained memory only.

The terminal observation accepts OFF only from a successful, valid snapshot with `domain_on == 0` and `(word & 0x90001cff) == 0`. This covers ACTUAL, TARGET, AUTO, RESET, DEV_DISABLE, PS_RESET and BUSY; historical bits 8 and 9 are ignored. Normal runtime-PM success alone cannot establish OFF. At most 101 post-call snapshots and 100 delays of at least 1,000 microseconds are requested. This bounds the observation loop, not the wall time of the synchronous PM call. Snapshot errors terminate the loop, negative PM results are preserved even if OFF is observed, and apparent OFF with inconsistent suspended/usage-zero accounting returns an error.

The remaining limits are physical OFF coverage, actual kernel/genpd concurrency and provider lifetime, cache and fabric drain, shared-memory coherence and cessation of all GPU DMA. Stable retained DRAM or an OFF word cannot authorize page unmapping, consumer destruction, module unload or restart.
