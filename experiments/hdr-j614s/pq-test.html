Experimental PQ grayscale ramp

Enable HDR for the internal panel in Plasma first. The vertical bands represent, left to right, 0.01, 1, 10, 50, 100, 200, 400 and 600 cd/m², encoded in PQ and BT.2020. They are intended to be neutral gray.

Use the fullscreen control for the image. Seeing bands does not prove calibrated luminance or HDR decoding. If the browser decodes in SDR, compare with an HDR-capable player before drawing conclusions. The candidate uses synthetic, approximate display metadata.
